Backend of **Chromascope** (BCS32 thesis): ingredient safety filter (Bayesian-Chromascope Hybrid), CIEDE2000 shade
matching, product catalog and personal color analysis. Used by the Chromascope web app; all `/api/` routes need a
signed-in user. Source: https://github.com/cinedalida/Chromascope (backend/). Product photos belong to their brands
and retailers and are shown for research purposes.
